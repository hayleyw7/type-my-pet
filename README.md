# Type My Pet

Ever wonder what your pet’s personality type would be? **Type My Pet** is a short, fun quiz that gives your cat, dog, or any companion a four-letter “personality type” in the same style as Myers-Briggs, based on how they actually act, not how you wish they would.

**[Take the quiz →](https://typemypet.surge.sh)**

## How to play

1. Open the link above and tap **Begin**.
2. You’ll see four questions. Each one has two choices. Pick the side that sounds more like your pet.
3. At the end, you’ll get a four-letter result (for example, **ENFP** or **ISTJ**).
4. Tap **Restart** to try again, or run it for another pet.

## What the questions mean

You’re not picking “good” or “bad”, just what fits best.

- **Shy or social?** Do they hide and lounge, or cling, play, and get the zoomies?
- **Routine or curious?** Do they love the same schedule, or get bored and explore?
- **Sweet or independent?** Are they cuddly and in your feelings, or cool and do their own thing?
- **Scheduled or spontaneous?** Do they want meals and walks on time, or go with the flow?

Your answers combine into one four-letter type at the end.

## A quick note

This is just for fun, not a real scientific test. Whatever result you get, your pet is still the best.
